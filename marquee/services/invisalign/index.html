Invisalign

Invisalign straightens your teeth with clear, removable trays — no metal, no guesswork on cost. We scan your teeth, show you the plan and the final result on screen, and give you one number before you decide. Most cases run 6 to 18 months depending on how far your teeth have to move.

How it works

Three steps, no mystery

  1. 01

    We take a 3D scan — no goopy molds — and map out the whole plan on screen so you can see the end result before you commit.

  2. 02

    You wear each set of clear trays for about two weeks, swapping at home. Most people come in every six to eight weeks for a quick check.

  3. 03

    When you finish, we make you a retainer to keep things in place. That's it.

What to expect

From scan to done

  1. Week 0Scan and plan. You see the predicted result and get the exact price.
  2. Week 12Teeth are visibly moving. Most people are well into their tray sets and past the awkward part.
  3. DoneFinal trays off, retainer on. Usually somewhere between month 6 and month 18.
