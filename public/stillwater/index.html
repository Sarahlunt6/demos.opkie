comfort menu

How we keep you comfortable

Explore the ways we help you relax, at your own pace. Open anything you like, close it when you are done. Nothing here is timed.

  • Nitrous oxide

    A light, breathable calm you receive through a soft nose piece. It clears within minutes, so you can drive yourself home.

    what it feels like
    A warm, floaty ease settles over you within a minute or two. You stay awake and aware, just softened and far less tense.
    who it’s for
    A gentle first step if you are nervous but want to stay present.
    Learn more about nitrous oxide→
  • Oral sedation

    A prescribed pill you take before your visit, so you arrive already relaxed.

    what it feels like
    A heavy, drowsy calm, like the edge of a nap. Many people remember very little afterward.
    who it’s for
    Moderate nerves, or a longer visit you would rather not recall in detail.
    Learn more about oral sedation→
  • IV sedation

    Sedation given gently through a vein and adjusted in real time by a trained team.

    what it feels like
    A deep, peaceful drift. Most of the appointment simply does not register, and time passes quickly.
    who it’s for
    Higher anxiety, longer treatment, or anyone who would rather sleep through it.
    Learn more about iv sedation→
  • Noise-cancelling headphones

    Over-ear headphones with your music, a show, or quiet — the sounds of the office disappear.

    what it feels like
    The room goes quiet. The drill and the chatter fade into whatever you would rather hear.
    who it’s for
    Anyone soothed by sound, or unsettled by the noises of a dental office.
  • Weighted blankets

    A soft, weighted blanket rests over you in the chair for as long as you like.

    what it feels like
    A steady, grounding weight, like a long hug, that helps your body settle.
    who it’s for
    Anyone who feels safer with gentle, even pressure.
  • Stop-anytime hand signal

    We agree on a simple hand signal before we begin. You raise it, and everything stops.

    what it feels like
    Control, returned to you. The moment your hand goes up we pause — no questions, no hesitation.
    who it’s for
    Everyone. This is how every visit here works.
  • Judgment-free policy

    No lectures about the past, no guilt, no sighs over your chart. The time that has passed is simply not a topic.

    what it feels like
    Relief. You can exhale and just be a person getting care.
    who it’s for
    Anyone who has been made to feel ashamed at a dentist before.
your first step

How your first step works

  1. 1

    Call or message

    Reach out however feels easiest — phone, text, or the form. A real person answers, and there is no script to get through.

  2. 2

    Talk with the doctor

    We talk first. No chair, no exam, no plan you have to agree to. Just a conversation about where you are and what you are afraid of.

  3. 3

    Decide together

    When you are ready, and only then, we map out a pace that works for you. You set it, and you can change it any time.
