an easy first visit

Gentle Cleanings

A cleaning is how many of our patients ease back in. We go slowly, narrate what we are doing, and pause whenever you need. Comfort options are on the table from the start, and there is no exam agenda hiding behind it — just a calm, thorough cleaning and a chance to get comfortable with us.

common questions

Good to know

What if it has been years and I am embarrassed?

That is the most common way our patients arrive, and it is genuinely fine here. There are no comments about the past — only a gentle cleaning and a fresh start.

Can I have nitrous for just a cleaning?

Yes, and many people do. Nitrous keeps a cleaning calm and easy, and you can still drive yourself home afterward.

Will it hurt?

We work gently and stop the moment anything is uncomfortable. If your gums are sensitive, we can numb the area or break it into shorter visits.

Whenever you are ready

There is no wrong way to start, and no pressure once you do. Reach out however feels easiest.
