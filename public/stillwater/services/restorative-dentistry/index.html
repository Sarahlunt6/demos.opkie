Restorative Dentistry

When a tooth needs repair, we fix it without drama. Fillings, crowns, and bonding are handled in unhurried visits, with whatever comfort options help you relax. We explain each step before it happens, and we stop whenever you need a moment. There is no rush to do everything at once.

common questions

Good to know

Can restorative work be done with sedation?

Yes. From nitrous to IV sedation, any of our comfort options can be paired with restorative treatment. We will talk through what fits your nerves and your visit.

Do I have to fix everything in one go?

No. We can break treatment into small, manageable steps and space them however you like. You set the pace, and there is no pressure to move faster than feels right.

Will you tell me what it costs first?

Always. You receive a clear written estimate before anything begins, and we check your insurance so there are no surprises.

Whenever you are ready

There is no wrong way to start, and no pressure once you do. Reach out however feels easiest.
