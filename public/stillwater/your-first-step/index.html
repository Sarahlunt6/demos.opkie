your first step

It starts with a conversation

If you have been putting this off, this is the page for you. Here is exactly what happens if you reach out, and everything you will never be asked to do.

  1. 1

    Call or message

    Reach out however feels easiest — phone, text, or the form. A real person answers, and there is no script to get through.

  2. 2

    Talk with the doctor

    We talk first. No chair, no exam, no plan you have to agree to. Just a conversation about where you are and what you are afraid of.

  3. 3

    Decide together

    When you are ready, and only then, we map out a pace that works for you. You set it, and you can change it any time.

no matter what

What you will never be asked to do

  • You will never be asked to justify how long it has been.

  • You will never be talked into treatment on the spot.

  • You will never be made to feel rushed or ashamed.

  • You can keep your headphones in the entire time.

if you would rather just talk

If even booking an exam feels like too much, you can start with a conversation and nothing else. Many of our patients do exactly that — they talk first, sometimes more than once, before they ever sit in the chair.

You can just say: “It has been a long time, and I am nervous.” We will take it from there.

honest answers

The questions people actually ask

Will I be judged for how long it has been?

No. We built this practice for people who have waited years. Whatever the reason, it is welcome here, and it stays between us. The time that has passed is simply not a topic.

Does the first conversation cost anything?

The first talk is free, by phone or in person. There is no charge to walk through your options and your worries before you decide anything.

What if I cry?

That happens here, and it is completely okay. We pause, get you a glass of water, and go at your pace. There is no wrong way to feel in this room.

What if it has been ten years or more?

Then you are in good company — many of our patients waited that long or longer. We start with whatever feels manageable and build slowly from there.

Whenever you are ready

There is no wrong way to start, and no pressure once you do. Reach out however feels easiest.
