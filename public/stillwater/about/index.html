why we built a calm practice

The quietest room in dentistry

credentials
  • DDS — University of the Pacific
  • Certified in oral and IV sedation
  • Member, American Dental Society of Anesthesiology

Stillwater exists because of a pattern Dr. Reyes saw again and again: people who had been frightened away from the dentist, often years before, and who carried that fear quietly. They were not difficult patients. They were people who had been hurt or rushed or shamed, and who had decided, understandably, to simply stop going.

So we built the practice around the opposite of all that. The lighting is warm and low. The pace is slow on purpose. The first thing that happens is a conversation, not a chair. And every comfort and sedation option is on the table from the start, because feeling in control is the whole point.

We are not for everyone, and that is fine. We are for the person who has been putting this off, who feels a knot in their stomach reading this, who is not sure they can do it. You can. We will help, gently, and only as fast as you want to go.

Dr. Nathan Reyes, DDS
