Veneers & Bonding

Veneers and bonding fix chips, gaps, and worn edges and can reshape a whole smile. Bonding is the quick, lower-cost option; veneers are the durable, made-to-measure one. Veneers run $950 to $1,400 per tooth, and you'll see the per-tooth math before you commit.

How it works

Three steps, no mystery

  1. 01

    We talk through what you want to change and design it on screen first, matched to your face and your other teeth.

  2. 02

    For veneers, we prep the teeth and fit your custom set; bonding is usually done in a single visit, freehand.

  3. 03

    You approve the look before it's final, and you know the per-tooth cost before we start.

Bonding is faster and cheaper and great for small fixes. Veneers last longer and look more uniform across several teeth. We'll tell you honestly which fits your case and budget.
