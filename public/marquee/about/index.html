the studio

Dentistry that talks like a person

Marquee is one studio, not a chain. We kept it small on purpose: same dentist every visit, no rotating cast, no quotas pushing anyone toward work they don't need. The space is modern because the tools are — same-day crowns, digital scans, screens you can actually see your own teeth on — but the point was never the gadgets. It's that you walk out knowing what happened and what it cost.

  • Noise-cancelling headphones and your show on the screen above the chair
  • Same-day crowns, so it's one visit instead of two
  • Real espresso in the waiting area, not a sad pot of coffee
  • Text us — a person answers, usually within the hour
