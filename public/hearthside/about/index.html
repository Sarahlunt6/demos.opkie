Our Story

A neighborhood practice, on purpose

Hearthside has cared for Cedar Grove families since 2006. We are not a chain and never plan to be — just a steady team that learns your name and watches your kids grow up.

Dr. Mark Bennett opened Hearthside in 2006 in the same Maple Avenue building it occupies today. He wanted a practice where families could relax — where the front desk knew your name, the hygienist remembered your last visit, and no one was rushed out the door.

Almost twenty years later, that is still the whole idea. Many of the children who came in for their first checkups now bring their own kids. The team has grown, but the feel has not: steady, warm, and on your side.

We keep the schedule a little lighter than most offices so every visit has room to breathe. We explain what we see with photos, check your insurance before you arrive, and tell you honestly when something can wait. That is how neighbors treat neighbors.
