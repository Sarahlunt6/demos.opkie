Straightening

Invisalign

Invisalign straightens teeth with clear, removable aligners — no metal brackets. It is a favorite with our teen and adult patients because it fits real life: you take the aligners out to eat and to clean your teeth. We plan and supervise every case in-house, so you see the same familiar team start to finish.

Common Questions

Good to know

Is my teen a good candidate?

Often, yes. Invisalign works well for many teens with mild to moderate crowding or spacing, and the removable aligners are easier to keep clean than braces. We will tell you honestly at the consultation.

How much does Invisalign cost?

Most cases here range from $4,000 to $6,000 depending on how much movement is needed. We give you a fixed quote, check your orthodontic benefits, and offer monthly payment plans.

How long does treatment take?

Simple cases can finish in six months; more involved ones take twelve to eighteen. Wearing the aligners as directed is the biggest factor.
