For Kids

Children's Dentistry

Children's dentistry at Hearthside is about building trust, not just checking teeth. We see kids from their first birthday, narrate everything before we do it, and let nervous little ones set the pace. Parents stay right beside their child the whole time. The goal is a kid who is not afraid of the dentist — for life.

For Parents

How we keep it calm

Written for parents: here is how we make the dentist easy for little ones — and what you can do to help.

  • We narrate everything before we do it, in kid-friendly words.

  • You stay right beside your child for the whole visit.

  • Headphones, sunglasses, and breaks whenever they need one.

  • Nitrous (laughing gas) is available for kids who need extra help relaxing.

  • A treasure chest at the end — every visit ends on a high note.

Common Questions

Good to know

What age should my child first see a dentist?

By their first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth. Early visits are short and friendly — mostly about getting comfortable and giving you guidance on brushing and snacks.

Can I stay with my child during the visit?

Always. You are welcome to stay right beside your child for the entire appointment. For many kids, that is exactly what makes it easy.

What if my child is scared?

That is completely normal, and we are good at it. We go slowly, name every tool, and never force anything. If today is just about sitting in the chair, that is a win.

Do you offer comfort options for anxious kids?

Yes — from simple things like headphones and breaks to nitrous (laughing gas) when it helps. We will talk through what is right for your child before anything begins.
