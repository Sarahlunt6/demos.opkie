How It Works

Your implant journey, step by step

Most patients want to know exactly what's involved before they start. Here's what each stage looks like, how long it takes, and what to expect.

We take a low-dose 3D scan of your jaw and review it with you on screen, confirming bone volume and the exact position for the implant. You leave with a written plan and a cost estimate.

Meet Your Doctor

Founder — Implant & Reconstructive Dentistry

Dr. Okafor founded Meridian in 2012 to build a practice around guided, digitally planned implant care. He has placed and restored more than 4,000 implants and teaches guided full-arch technique to other clinicians. He plans every case from a 3D scan before a single instrument is used.
