What you’re in for, start to finish

The biggest unknown for most implant patients is the process itself. Step through it below — what happens at each stage, how long it takes, and what you feel.

Step 01 / 04 — 1 VISIT — 60 MIN

Consultation & 3D Scan

We take a low-dose 3D scan of your jaw and review it with you on screen, confirming bone volume and the exact position for the implant. You leave with a written plan and a cost estimate.
