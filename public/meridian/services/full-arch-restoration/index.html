Full-Arch Restoration

Full-arch restoration replaces an entire upper or lower set of teeth with a fixed bridge anchored on four to six implants. Unlike a denture, it does not come out and does not cover the roof of your mouth. We plan the implant positions and the final teeth digitally before surgery, and you leave the placement appointment with a fixed temporary arch.

/ Candidacy

You’re likely a candidate if

  • You are missing most or all of the teeth in an arch, or facing full extraction.

  • You wear a denture that moves, clicks, or limits what you can eat.

  • You want fixed teeth that stay in place and do not cover your palate.

  • You are ready to plan a half-day procedure and a few months of healing.

/ Common Questions

Good to know

How much does full-arch (All-on-4 style) treatment cost?

Fixed full-arch treatment at Meridian ranges from $22,000 to $32,000 per arch, quoted as a single fixed price after your 3D scan — including surgery, the same-day temporary, and the final prosthesis. Monthly financing is available.

Will I leave with teeth the same day?

Yes. A fixed temporary arch is placed the same day as surgery, so you never leave without teeth. The final, stronger prosthesis is fitted after the implants have integrated.

Is it painful?

The procedure is done under local anesthetic with sedation options, and most patients manage afterward with over-the-counter medication and a few days of soft foods. We review exactly what to expect before the day.

How is this different from dentures?

A full-arch bridge is fixed to implants — it does not come out, does not slip, and does not cover the roof of your mouth, so you taste and feel food normally. A denture rests on the gums and moves.

How long does the whole process take?

From placement to the final prosthesis is typically four to six months. You wear fixed temporary teeth throughout, so you are never without a smile.

Does insurance or financing help?

We quote a single fixed price and verify any dental or medical benefits that may apply. Monthly financing through CareCredit and Proceed Finance can spread the cost, including extended terms for qualifying plans.
