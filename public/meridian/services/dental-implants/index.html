Dental Implants

A dental implant replaces the root of a missing tooth with a titanium post, then carries a custom ceramic crown. It is the only option that stands on its own without altering the teeth beside it. Every implant at Meridian is planned from a 3D scan and placed with a surgical guide, so the position is decided before treatment begins — not during it.

/ Candidacy

You’re likely a candidate if

  • You are missing one or more teeth, or have a tooth that cannot be saved.

  • You want a replacement that functions like a natural tooth and does not come out.

  • You have enough jawbone, or are open to grafting to rebuild it (we confirm this from your 3D scan).

  • You are in reasonable general health and do not smoke heavily.

/ Common Questions

Good to know

How much do dental implants cost in Frisco?

A single implant with its abutment and crown typically runs $3,800 to $6,000 at Meridian, depending on whether grafting is needed. You receive a written, fixed price after your 3D scan, and monthly financing is available through CareCredit and Proceed Finance.

Does getting an implant hurt?

Placement is done under local anesthetic and most patients are surprised by how comfortable it is — often easier than an extraction. Mild soreness for a few days afterward is managed with over-the-counter medication. Sedation is available if you would prefer it.

How long do dental implants last?

The implant post can last a lifetime with good care. The crown on top may need replacing after 10 to 15 years, like any restoration. Daily cleaning and regular checkups are what protect it.

What if I don't have enough bone?

Your 3D scan tells us precisely how much bone is available. When it is insufficient, a graft can rebuild the site — often at the same appointment as placement. We will show you exactly what your case needs.

How long does the whole process take?

From placement to final crown is usually three to six months, most of it healing time during which you wear a temporary. Some cases allow a faster timeline; your plan will tell you which applies.

Does insurance cover implants?

Coverage varies. Many plans contribute toward the crown even when they exclude the implant itself. We verify your benefits before you start and document the treatment so you receive every dollar you are entitled to.
