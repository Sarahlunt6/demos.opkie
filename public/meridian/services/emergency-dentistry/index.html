Emergency Dentistry

Dental pain, swelling, and trauma do not wait. Meridian holds same-day appointments for emergencies — a cracked or knocked-out tooth, a lost crown, severe pain, or swelling. We diagnose the problem quickly with digital imaging and get you out of pain first, then plan any further treatment.

/ Common Questions

Good to know

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe or worsening pain, facial swelling, a knocked-out or badly broken tooth, a lost crown or filling, or bleeding that will not stop. If you are unsure, call us — we will tell you how quickly you need to be seen.

Can you see me today?

We reserve same-day slots for emergencies. Call as early as you can; if we cannot reach you by phone, our scheduling line will direct urgent cases.

What should I do if my tooth is knocked out?

Handle it by the crown, not the root, gently rinse it, and if possible place it back in the socket or in milk, then come in immediately. Time matters for saving the tooth.

What will an emergency visit cost?

An emergency exam with imaging is $150, credited toward treatment if you proceed. We give you a clear estimate for any treatment before starting and verify insurance where possible.

Do you treat pain before doing other work?

Yes. Our first priority is getting you out of pain and stabilizing the problem. Any larger plan — a crown, root canal, or implant — is discussed afterward, once you are comfortable.
