/ SAME-DAY CROWNS

Same-Day Crowns

Same-day crowns use an in-house mill and a digital scan to design and fabricate a ceramic crown while you wait — no putty impressions, no temporary, and no second appointment. The result is a precise, tooth-colored restoration fitted the same day.

/ Common Questions

Good to know

Are same-day crowns as strong as lab crowns?

Yes. They are milled from the same high-strength ceramic blocks used by dental labs and bonded the same way. The difference is the turnaround, not the quality.

What does a crown cost?

A same-day ceramic crown typically runs $1,200 to $1,600 depending on the tooth. We provide a clear estimate before treatment and verify your insurance benefits.

How long does the appointment take?

Most same-day crowns are completed in about two hours, including design and milling. You leave with the final crown bonded in place.

Will it match my other teeth?

The crown is shade-matched to your neighboring teeth and milled from tooth-colored ceramic, so it blends in. Dr. Whitfield adjusts the fit and bite before you leave.

Does insurance cover crowns?

Most dental plans cover a portion of medically necessary crowns. We verify your benefits and give you a clear estimate of your share before any treatment.
