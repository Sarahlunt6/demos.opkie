/ Patient Info

Before your first visit

Everything you need before your first visit — what to expect, how billing works, and the forms to complete ahead of time.

/ First Visit

What to expect

  • Plan for about an hour. A first visit includes digital imaging, an exam, and a conversation about your goals and options.
  • For implant consultations we include a 3D scan so we can plan precisely and give you a written estimate the same day.
  • Bring a list of medications and any recent dental records or images from another office.
/ Insurance

How billing works

  • We work with most PPO dental plans and file your claims for you as a courtesy.
  • We verify your benefits before treatment and give you a written estimate of your portion — no surprises.
  • We are not a managed-care (HMO) office, which lets us keep appointments unhurried and plan treatment around what you need.
/ Financing

Paying over time

  • Treatment is quoted as a fixed, written price before anything begins.
  • Monthly financing is available through CareCredit and Proceed Finance, including extended interest-free terms for qualifying plans.
  • For larger cases we will lay out the full investment and the monthly options side by side, so the decision is clear.
/ Forms

Paperwork, done ahead

  • New-patient forms are sent by secure link when you schedule, so you can complete them before you arrive.
  • If you prefer, arrive fifteen minutes early and our team will help you complete them in the office.
Ready to Start?

Let's plan your treatment together

Your consultation includes 3D imaging, a thorough exam, and a clear treatment plan with costs — so you know exactly what to expect.
